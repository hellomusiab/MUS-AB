Case Study 01

The Debut Thriller Breakthrough

The Context: A fiction author launched a debut mystery thriller with zero preexisting audience. Despite glowing early reviews, the book was languishing past rank 200,000 on Amazon with less than three sales per week.

The Strategy: I conducted a complete audit of the listing. We rebuilt the subtitle to incorporate high intent reader search terms, isolated six micro subgenres for category repositioning, and launched a laser focused Amazon Sponsored Products campaign targeting readers of top genre authors.

The Result: Within fourteen days, the book broke into the top 100 overall Kindle store in its country, secured three #1 New Release badges, and sustained an average of forty eight paid sales per day over the following four months.

Case Study 02

Non Fiction Authority Positioning

The Context: A corporate leadership consultant authored a comprehensive business book designed to generate consulting leads. The book was losing money on advertising due to poor sales page conversion.

The Strategy: I completely overhauled the sales page copywriting using problem agitator solution frameworks, designed custom A+ visual content highlighting key framework takeaways, and restructured backend keywords to capture high value enterprise search traffic.

The Result: The sales conversion rate increased from 3.2% to 14.8%. Ad spend dropped by 45% while total volume tripled, leading directly to five high ticket client contracts acquired through the book listing.
