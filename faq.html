Frequently Asked Questions

Clear answers regarding operational policies, project timelines, ad budget expectations, and communication models.

How do we communicate during a campaign?

I operate one hundred percent exclusively via email at hellomusiab@gmail.com. I do not take phone calls, video meetings, or visual check ins. This policy ensures every detail, strategy, and data point is documented in writing, allowing me to focus entirely on executing your campaigns without distraction.

Do you guarantee a #1 Bestseller tag?

No honest marketer can guarantee a badge because Amazon controls the final algorithm parameters. What I guarantee is a statistically superior process that optimizes every variable under your control to give your book the absolute highest chance of hitting top rankings.

What is the minimum recommended ad spend?

I recommend starting with a minimum ad budget of 5 to 10 dollars per day per book. This provides enough data for Amazon's algorithm to learn and allows us to make statistically valid adjustments to lower your cost per acquisition.

How long before I see real results?

While initial ad impressions and clicks begin within 48 hours, a fully optimized campaign foundation takes 30 to 60 days to stabilize and yield consistent, organic momentum.
